# Piano samples

Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm (a Yamaha C5), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
(LICENSE-CC-BY-3.0.txt, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Taken from the SFZ Instruments
distribution, https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano (commit 3382bf9).

Changes made for Notations: one middle velocity layer (v8 of 16) of the 30 sampled notes, A0 to C8 in minor
thirds; mixed to mono; cut to 3 seconds with a 0.8-second fade; converted to AAC at about 96 kbps (.m4a).
Notes in between are played by changing the playback rate of the nearest sample.
